PCB compatibility — check before ordering

The selected Cotubex Velleman BH-001 holder has solder terminals. The existing design was based on a Keystone 3034 holder footprint. These are not assumed to be footprint-compatible. Update/check the holder footprint and its pad positions in KiCad, then verify the 3D/2D placement and PCB connections before sending the board to manufacture.
